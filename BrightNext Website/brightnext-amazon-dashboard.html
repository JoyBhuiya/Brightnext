Connect Amazon Seller Central (SP-API) ◉ DEMO
Link your Amazon Seller account for real-time data — replaces demo data with live sales
1
Register App
Create an app in Amazon Seller Central → Apps & Services → Develop Apps. Get your Client ID and Client Secret.
2
Authorise Access
Grant BrightNext dashboard OAuth access to your Seller account. We request read-only: Orders, Inventory, Finance, and Reports scopes.
3
Go Live
Once authorised, the dashboard fetches live data via SP-API endpoints. Auto-refresh every 15 minutes. All data encrypted in transit.
